# Code of Conduct

versaDeploy follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, in Spanish or English. Assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@kriollo](https://github.com/kriollo)). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do, following the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant.
